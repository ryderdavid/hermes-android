# Hermes (Material You fork) v2.1.31-you.22

Approval-card cold-open fix + keyboard inset fix, on top of [v2.1.31-you.21](v2.1.31-you.21.md).

## Fixed

- **Approval card no longer vanishes on cold open from a notification.** A
  cold-launch tap was delivered twice (launch details + response callback) and
  the snackbar Review / share / quick-chat paths could also push over a
  notification-opened chat, covering the approval sheet until back was
  pressed. Notification routes are now tracked with a root `NavigatorObserver`
  and never stacked twice; share/quick-chat auto-nav bails when a notification
  route is already on the stack. (hmy-13e)
- **Keyboard: back-button IME dismissal no longer leaves the composer floating
  mid-screen.** Dismissing the IME with system back keeps field focus, which
  defeated the focus-based stale-inset clamp; `KeyboardInsetGuard` now also
  unfocuses the editable on a back press that no route consumed, so the stuck
  `viewInsets.bottom` is clamped and the composer docks. (hmy-15m)

## Tests

- `flutter analyze` clean; full `flutter test` green (2160 passed), including
  new cold-launch dedupe, Review/share/quick-chat non-stacking, and
  back-button inset regression tests.
